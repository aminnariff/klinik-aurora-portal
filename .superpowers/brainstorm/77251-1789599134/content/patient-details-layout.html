Option A: Modern Centered Modal (640px) — Recommended

Option B: Slide-over Side Drawer (520px)

Slides in from right side without obscuring patient list. High efficiency for rapid browsing across multiple patient records.

Wide command modal uniting Patient Info, Appointment History, Points Ledger, and Clinic Notes in dedicated tab panels.

Option A Advantages (Modal)

  • Consistent with existing portal modals (no layout shift)
  • Spacious 2-column layout prevents vertical scrolling fatigue
  • Instant visual feedback for derived NRIC data (DOB, age, gender)
  • Works cleanly on both desktop and tablet screens

Option B Advantages (Drawer)

  • Keeps the patient table visible in background
  • Fast if clinic staff need to click patient after patient quickly
  • More compact, but narrower form fields
